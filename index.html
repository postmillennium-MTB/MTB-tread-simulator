How to interpret this

The equations below are intentionally simplified heuristics. Scores help visualize trade-offs; they are not validated predictions of real-world grip or tire safety.

The source model does not include temperature, casing construction, rider skill, or measured tire-specific compound data. Change one input at a time to explore the model’s assumed relationships.
Conceptual simulator; not a certified tire-testing instrument. Ride within the tire and rim manufacturers’ pressure limits.
